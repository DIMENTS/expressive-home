See your whole home at a glance, on the tablet on the wall, on your laptop and on your phone. Expressive Home makes a dashboard from your own devices and rooms the moment you open it, and you can arrange it the way you like. Locks, garage doors and alarms ask to be held or confirmed, so nothing opens by accident.

Scan a code in the settings of the app to add a device. Everything stays on your own network and keeps working without internet. For Homey Pro.
